Privacy Policy & Terms

This is an independent prototype / demo project. It is not an official website of Mira Bhayandar Municipal Corporation and complaints are not forwarded to MBMC.

What we collect

  • Ward, location/landmark, category, urgency, description and an optional photo. These are public in the tracking ledger.
  • Name and mobile number (optional). These are stored separately and are visible only to authorised staff. Filing anonymously stores no name or phone.

Why

Only to demonstrate how a grievance tracking system works. No SMS or calls are made, and data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Your responsibilities

Do not write phone numbers, addresses of private persons or other personal details in the description. Do not upload photos showing faces or vehicle number plates.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in a Supabase (cloud) database. To request deletion of your report, write to the project owner at: [your email here].
